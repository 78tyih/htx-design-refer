# HTX Medium Router

## Source-grounded media

### `poster-social`

Primary families:
- `brand-blue-field`
- `blue-grid-reward`
- `soft-blue-information`
- `vip-gold`
- `huobao-character`

Selection logic:
- large reward / percentage / prize → `blue-grid-reward`
- recruitment / community / product introduction → `soft-blue-information`
- generic campaign / launch → `brand-blue-field`
- VIP / institution → `vip-gold`
- mascot-led consumer campaign → `huobao-character`

### `longform-operation`

Primary family:
- `blue-white-hero`

Use clear Hero/content partition, strong black body copy on white, restrained dividers and limited section decoration.

### `huobao-fast`

Use only `huobao-character`.

### `review-only`

Review against brand tokens, safe areas, logo placement, copy fidelity, family fidelity and anti-identity rules.

## Derived adapters

These are extrapolated from the supplied design system. They are not claimed as source-authored HTX rules.

### `web-ui`

Recommended:
- `blue-white-hero` for landing pages and service pages;
- `brand-blue-field` for hero areas;
- `vip-gold` for premium/VIP modules only;
- `soft-blue-information` for onboarding/information states.

Preserve usability. Do not reproduce poster-style oversized text where it harms interface hierarchy.

### `ppt-pdf`

Recommended rhythm:
- title / section divider: `brand-blue-field` or `vip-gold`;
- content / evidence: white field with black copy and brand-blue accents;
- multi-module pages: borrow `blue-white-hero` and section-rule logic.

### `infographic`

Use white/light field, black body copy, brand-blue highlight, no unnecessary shadows, no invented ornament. Use semantic diagrams before decorative icons.
