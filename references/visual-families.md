# HTX Visual Families

The six families below are distilled from the user-supplied HTX brand/social/Huobao Skills.

## 1. `brand-blue-field` — 品牌蓝场

**Source:** `htx-brand.skill` default `brand-blue` theme.

**Identity**
- full HTX brand-blue field or blue gradient;
- strong left-aligned title hierarchy;
- white or black copy chosen by local contrast;
- minimal decoration;
- campaign first, ornament second.

**Best for**
- standard campaign posters;
- event launches;
- product announcements;
- simple reward or invitation campaigns.

**Avoid**
- random grid decoration;
- neon;
- glass cards;
- extra illustrations when none are supplied.

---

## 2. `blue-grid-reward` — 蓝色栅格奖励

**Source:** `htx-social-poster.skill` `big-number-grid` + `blue-grid-gradient-01`.

**Identity**
- supplied blue grid / light-column background;
- left side kept calmer for text;
- strong reward/value hierarchy;
- black title and content in light regions;
- background itself carries the visual theme.

**Best for**
- reward amount;
- fee rebate;
- percentage benefit;
- trading competition;
- prize pool;
- multi-step campaign with no character image.

**Avoid**
- right-aligned long copy;
- full-width tables;
- dense long-form information;
- stretching the background;
- placing body copy over the right-side light-column structure.

---

## 3. `soft-blue-information` — 柔和蓝白信息

**Source:** `htx-social-poster.skill` `soft-information` + `soft-blue-white-01`.

**Identity**
- white-to-blue soft field;
- calm, friendly, information-first tone;
- title at upper-left;
- image or information object around mid-frame;
- supporting date/info near the lower area;
- user-supplied images may be integrated without distortion.

**Best for**
- recruitment;
- community;
- product introduction;
- gentle announcement;
- information posters.

**Avoid**
- excessive optical effects;
- extra light beams;
- high-density rule content;
- stretching user images.

---

## 4. `blue-white-hero` — 蓝白分区 Hero

**Source:** `htx-brand.skill` default long-form / split-layout guidance.

**Identity**
- blue Hero on top + continuous white content field below;
- Hero normally dominates the vertical rhythm;
- information area supports, rather than overpowers, the Hero;
- black body copy on white;
- blue vertical rule only for genuine multi-module long-form sections.

**Best for**
- long-form operation graphics;
- information-heavy campaign explainers;
- rules, steps and multi-module content;
- PPT / PDF adaptation;
- landing-page sections.

**Source proportion guidance**
- Hero: approximately 52–58% when content fits;
- white information region: approximately 42–48%.

**Avoid**
- oversized white information area when content is sparse;
- unnecessary rounded cards;
- decorative geometry added simply to fill space.

---

## 5. `vip-gold` — VIP 金色

**Source:** `htx-brand.skill` `vip-gold` theme.

**Identity**
- restrained gold gradient `#DEC48A → #F5ECD7`;
- gold core `#C9A84C`;
- white support color;
- black body text on light fields;
- gold left rule for structured modules;
- premium but not ornamental-luxury overload.

**Best for**
- institutional communication;
- VIP benefits;
- high-value services;
- premium membership or partnership materials.

**Avoid**
- black/gold luxury clichés;
- metallic 3D excess;
- ornate borders;
- serif-for-luxury assumptions not supported by the source.

---

## 6. `huobao-character` — 火宝角色活动

**Source:** `htx-huobao-fast.skill` and Huobao collaboration rules in `htx-brand.skill`.

**Identity**
- blue gradient campaign field;
- left text-safe area;
- Huobao character in the middle-right / lower-right;
- official logo in upper-right;
- core reward numbers can use `#FFC800`;
- character action and props carry the campaign narrative.

**Best for**
- consumer-facing campaigns;
- reward mechanics;
- referral / social activities;
- themed promotional visuals.

**Avoid**
- institutional decks;
- dense rules pages;
- adding network nodes / orbit lines / floating UI badges;
- inventing mascot costume changes or unsupported character identity features.
