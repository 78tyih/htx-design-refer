# HTX Brand DNA

## Source-grounded identity

The supplied skills consistently describe HTX design as **clear, commercial, legible and disciplined** rather than decorative or cinematic.

The strongest recurring traits are:

- HTX blue used as a structural field;
- left-aligned campaign hierarchy;
- oversized benefit/value emphasis when the content warrants it;
- black text on white/light areas and white text on deep blue areas;
- fixed logo discipline in the upper-right;
- large safe areas and measured typography;
- limited font weights (400 / 500 / 700);
- restrained use of modules and dividers;
- no shadows, neon, dark dramatic backgrounds or invented ornamental graphics;
- content fidelity: do not rewrite the user's supplied copy by default.

## Color system

### Brand blue

- `#0066FF`
- gradient: `#0066FF → #2692FF`
- direction: vertical, dark/top to light/bottom

### VIP gold

- gold core: `#C9A84C`
- gradient: `#DEC48A → #F5ECD7`
- direction: vertical, dark/top to light/bottom

### Neutral system

- text black: `#000000`
- white: `#FFFFFF`
- divider: `#E8E8E8`
- VIP divider: `#E0D5C0`

### Huobao reward emphasis

- `#FFC800`
- reserved for core numeric reward expressions, currency signs, percentages and directly attached units in Huobao title systems.

## Typography system

Source skills specify:

- Latin and numerals: Urbanist
- Chinese: HarmonyOS Sans SC
- Regular: 400
- Medium: 500
- Bold: 700
- never 800/900/Black

The intended hierarchy is:

- primary title: 700
- secondary title: 500 or 400
- body: 400
- key data: 700

Use size and spacing before extra weight.

## Margin logic

For single-canvas social posters:

- target safe margin: about 80 px on 1080-wide canvas;
- hard minimum: 64 px;
- logo uses a 60 px top/right offset at 1080×1080;
- other sizes scale the same logic proportionally.

## Anti-identity

Avoid:

- generic blue-purple SaaS gradients;
- neon crypto styling;
- deep black dramatic backgrounds unless supplied by a specific approved system;
- glassmorphism;
- heavy shadows;
- random circles, lines, glow, grids or textures not present in approved assets;
- invented badges or marketing copy;
- italic typography;
- arbitrary use of 800/900 font weights;
- recoloring or distorting logo / mascot assets.
